# CHAMPS — notes

Reading: one-page Greek site for CHAMPS Kickboxing–Boxing Club. Audience: locals, adults and parents. One action: book a trial class (call / mailto).

## Inventory
- Name CHAMPS; logo is the wordmark inside ring ropes.
- Hexagon LED light panels on the ceiling (clusters of three), the signature of the room.
- Bright white/pale grey hall: grey floor, white walls, black pads and bags lettered CHAMPS in white.
- Full ring with white ropes, black corner posts lettered CHAMPS; ring mat printed "CHAMPS kickboxing – boxing".
- Red LED round timer on the wall.
- Mirror wall with an LED strip at the floor; mezzanine; glass street front in an apartment block.
- Timetable: Mon–Fri 10:30 mixed, 17:30 kids, 18:30 mixed, 19:30 cross training / boxing alternating, 20:30 mixed (Wed sparring). Sat 10:30 only. Sun closed. 26 classes a week.
- Slogan on their poster: "Train hard · Fight easy".
- Team photo: adults and kids together.
